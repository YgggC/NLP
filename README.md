# NLP Portfolio – Course Projects

## Overview

This repository contains a curated collection of Natural Language Processing projects completed throughout a semester-long course.
It covers both **classical NLP methods** and **modern deep learning approaches**, with additional exploration into **multilingual and multimodal settings**.

## Topics Covered

* Text preprocessing & normalization
* TF-IDF and statistical methods
* Hidden Markov Models (POS tagging)
* Syntactic parsing
* Topic modeling (Top2Vec)
* Multimodal models (LLaVA)
* Humor understanding in text
* Chinese word segmentation (independent exploration)

## Highlight Projects

### 1. Topic Modeling with Top2Vec

* Explored unsupervised topic discovery
* Prompt-based interpretation of topics

### 2. POS Tagging with HMM

* Implemented probabilistic sequence labeling
* Analyzed transition/emission probabilities

### 3. Multimodal Understanding with LLaVA

* Experimented with vision-language models
* Investigated prompt behavior under quantization (4-bit)

## Key Takeaways

* Strong understanding of both statistical and neural NLP methods
* Ability to analyze language across different linguistic systems (English & Chinese)
* Experience with experimental workflows in Jupyter notebooks
