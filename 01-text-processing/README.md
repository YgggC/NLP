# Text Processing

In this section, we explore various techniques to preprocess text data:

## Tokenization
The process of splitting text into individual words or tokens.

## Lowercasing
Converting all characters in the text to lowercase to maintain uniformity.

## Punctuation Removal
Removing punctuation marks from text, which can help in reducing noise during analysis.

## Stopword Removal
Filtering out common words (like 'and', 'the', etc.) that may not contribute to the meaning.

## Stemming
The process of reducing words to their base or root form.

## Lemmatization
Similar to stemming but considers the context and converts the word to its meaningful base form.
